What $24,000 includes

Yacht and captain. You shop. No chef, and no meals hiding in the number.

We publish two seven-night shapes on the same class of catamaran from Tortola. People mix them up. This is the cheaper one, written plainly.

In the $24,000 rate

Seven nights, up to eight guests. The yacht. Our captain. The boat’s fuel and the usual moorings for a Channel week, unless the brief turns into something unusual. You sleep on board. The captain runs the yacht and the safety of the week.

Not in the rate

A chef. Meals. A hosted bar. Your flights. Shore dinners. Park entry if you arrive like a tourist instead of on the boat. Anegada on a week the hull should not go. Anything that sounds like a hotel breakfast.

You provision — Friday on the ground, Saturday morning, or a list sent ahead. That is the point of the product. If you do not want to think about groceries, the other brochure is $36,000 with a chef who is not the captain.

How to read the two numbers

Captain-only is not a discounted all-inclusive. It is a different week. Crewed-house yachts with a published APA are a third thing again, and we will not name them until a commission is in writing.
