White Bay without the lunch rush

The beach is famous. The hour after the day boats leave is the one worth having.

White Bay on Jost Van Dyke is a short hop from Tortola’s west end. Sandy Cay sits next door. The photographs are real. So is the midday raft-up of shared boats and villa tenders.

When to be there

Late afternoon. The day products are turning for home. The light goes gold. You can still swim. You can still walk the sand. If you want a table, the desk asks before you fly — we do not wander in at peak and look surprised.

The night

Great Harbour if the group wants a village evening. A quieter hook on the same island if they do not. White Bay itself is a beach, not an overnight plan we force on a family with early sleepers.

What we will not do

Build a seven-night rate around one bar. Promise a private beach on a Saturday in February. Skip The Baths or Anegada because someone saw a t-shirt. Jost is a day in a good week. It is not the week.
