Privacy

How this desk handles a brief, a page view, and cookies.

Sunny Day Charters is a booking desk in Nanny Cay, Tortola, British Virgin Islands. Contact: hello@sunnydaycharters.com · WhatsApp +1 307-381-8011.

What we collect

When you inquire we keep the name, dates, party size, and notes you send — by form, email, or WhatsApp — so we can hold a boat. We do not sell that list.

Analytics

  • Simple Analytics counts visits without cookies or personal identifiers. It runs on every page.
  • Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-SN9HXBMR2D) measures which pages guests use to plan a week. IP addresses are anonymized.

Cookies

Google Analytics may set its own cookies to measure pages. Hosting (Netlify) and the CDN (Cloudflare) may set a technical cookie needed to deliver the page.

Forms and mail

Inquire forms go to Netlify, then to the desk inbox. WhatsApp messages stay in that thread. We keep briefs long enough to run the charter and handle a follow-up season, then delete what we no longer need.

Third parties

Partner day trips on Experiences may open a Viator listing. Their terms apply once you leave this site. Google, Netlify, Cloudflare, and WhatsApp process data under their own policies when you use those tools.

Your choices

Email the desk to see or correct an inquiry we hold. This page is a working notice for a small BVI desk, not a statute.
