In the water

A private day is built around the snorkel

The boat is the way out. The day is the time in clear water — a quiet pass over pale sand, and a route chosen for the morning rather than a fixed list of stops.

We keep the plan light until we see the weather. Then we pick the lee side and stay as long as the group wants to stay.
